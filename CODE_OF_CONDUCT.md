# Code of conduct

Mystic is made by people who care for the Earth, for each other and for the
future, and who share fairly. We expect the same here.

**Do:** be kind and specific; assume good intent; credit others' work; make
room for people new to open source; disagree about ideas, not people.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; post sexual or violent content;
share anyone's private information; pressure people to work for free; use the
project to promote anything that harms people or the planet.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent
the project.

**If something happens**, write to the maintainers privately through a
[security advisory](https://github.com/Earth-Care-Network/mystic/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches only them) with what happened and links. We'll respond within a
week, keep reporters' names private, and may warn, remove content, or ban
people from the project.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
